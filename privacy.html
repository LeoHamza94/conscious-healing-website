Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Conscious-Healing collects through this website, why we collect it, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have.

1. Who We Are

Conscious-Healing (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website. You can reach us at theconscioushealingpyramid@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information. If you become a member, we collect your name and email address and the password you choose. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, so we never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Contact messages. When you write to us through the Contact page, we receive your name, email address, and message. These are sent to us by email.
  • Email list. If you tick the optional email-list box when you sign up, your email address is stored with our email service provider, Kit.
  • Orders. When you buy from The Farmacy, we collect the name, shipping address, email address, and order details needed to fill your order. Payment is handled by a third-party payment processor. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Assessments. Answers you enter in the Energetic Assessment stay in your browser and are not sent to or stored by us. If you are signed in, your Chakra Assessment answers and results are saved to your account so you can view them later; if you are not signed in, they are not saved. You can ask us to delete saved results at any time.
  • Automatic information. Like most websites, we and our service providers receive technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you view. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) keeps basic server logs.

3. Please Do Not Send Medical Information

We do not need your diagnoses, medications, or medical history to answer a question or fill an order. Please do not include that kind of information in your messages or emails to us. Our content is educational and is not medical advice.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • to create and manage your account and give you access to members-only content;
  • to respond to your messages and questions;
  • to process, ship, and support your orders;
  • to send you emails you have signed up for;
  • to understand how the site is used and to improve it; and
  • to keep the site secure and to meet legal obligations.

5. Email

We send marketing or newsletter email only to people who have signed up for it. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to us at theconscioushealingpyramid@gmail.com and we will remove you. Account and order messages (such as a password reset or shipping notice) are not marketing and may still be sent.

6. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site and fill orders, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication) for sign-in;
  • Kit for our email list;
  • a third-party payment processor for payments;
  • shipping carriers for delivery; and
  • GitHub for website hosting.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights or the safety of others. Each provider handles information under its own privacy policy.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may stop sign-in from working.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including relying on established providers for sign-in and payments. No website or method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. How Long We Keep Information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

10. Your Choices

You may ask us to show you, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to unsubscribe you from email, by writing to theconscioushealingpyramid@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state’s privacy laws, and we will honor valid requests.

11. Children

This website is intended for adults. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Links to Other Sites

This site links to services we do not control, such as YouTube and TikTok. Their privacy practices are their own.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version here and change the “Last updated” date above.

14. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Write to us at theconscioushealingpyramid@gmail.com.
